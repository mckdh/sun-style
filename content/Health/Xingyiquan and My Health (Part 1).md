_Why learning to let go was the hardest part._

## Before I Started

Around 2016, before I began practicing Sun-style Xingyiquan, my body felt uncomfortable most of the time. Even without a fever, my chest and neck often felt hot and stuffy. My face also flushed red during ordinary chats at work or when meeting someone new, which made me self-conscious. On top of that, my mind felt restless and my upper body stayed stiff.

I had already practiced another style of Taijiquan, mostly memorizing forms. I could copy the movements, but I never learned how to drop the tension in my upper body while keeping a steady stance.

## A Boring First Year

When I joined my Xingyiquan group, I wanted to learn fighting techniques right away. Instead, my first year was mostly repetitive solo drills. Compared to the partner work that came later, it felt pretty dull at times.

Still, I was learning a basic rule: keep the upper body loose while building strength in the legs. It sounds easy until you try it. I did not even realize how tight I kept my shoulders until I had to let them drop.

At the same time, relaxing does not mean going limp like a wet noodle. If you drop all structure, your posture collapses. If you tense up, you get stiff. The real work is staying loose up top while your legs hold you steady, and I am still trying to get that balance right every time I train.

## What Changed for Me

Without expecting it, my daily health started to improve during that first year of basics.

* **The trapped heat faded:** As I learned to drop my shoulders, that stuffy, feverish feeling in my chest and neck slowly went away.
* **Less facial flushing:** My face stopped turning bright red during normal conversations, so I could finally talk to coworkers without worrying about how red my cheeks looked.
* **A calmer mind:** Letting go of physical stiffness and standing with a solid base helped my mind settle down too. Life still has stress, but I feel less rattled by daily hassles.

![[meme-drake-xingyiquan-health.png|480]]

None of this looks impressive on camera. You cannot show off a calm chest or a steady mood. Still, those quiet shifts made my everyday life much easier. Looking back, I am glad my teacher kept me on the basics instead of rushing into advanced moves.

## A Quick Note

This is just my personal experience, and I am not saying Xingyiquan is a medical cure for flushing, heat, or anxiety. If you have ongoing symptoms, you should always see a doctor.

For me, Sun-style Xingyiquan brought real comfort to my body and mind, and that is a big reason I have stuck with it for years.
